# Northstar CRM

## Core product

Northstar CRM is a fictional sales workspace for teams of 10–250 representatives. It includes contact management, pipeline stages, email templates, task reminders, and CSV import.

## Implementation

Standard implementation is fictional and takes 10 business days after the customer supplies clean CSV data. A dedicated sandbox is available only on the Enterprise plan.

## Integrations

The fictional product supports Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, and a documented REST API. It does not have a native SAP connector.
